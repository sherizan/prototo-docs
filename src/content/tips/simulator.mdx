The iPhone on the right of Prototo is a real iOS Simulator, running your prototype live. Prototo runs it for you in the background, so there's no separate Simulator window to manage.

## Screenshot

Get to the screen you want, then press **⌘⇧4**, then **Space**, and click the iPhone preview. macOS saves a picture of it to your **Desktop**, ready for Slack, Figma, or slides.

## Record a clip

Click **Record** in the top right, tap through your prototype, and click it again to stop. Your clip opens in Studio, wrapped in a 3D iPhone you can angle and export. See [Record and wrap](/studio/recording).

## Show where you tap

The round touch cursor on the preview shows exactly where you click, which helps when you present. Turn it on or off with **View › Show Touch Cursor**. It only appears in the live preview: recordings capture the phone screen itself.

## Full screen

Use the full-screen button in the toolbar under the iPhone to give the preview the whole window, for presenting or a closer look. Press **Esc** to get the agent back.

## If the preview stops responding

If taps stop landing but the picture keeps moving, choose **View › Restart Preview**. Your prototype comes straight back. More fixes in [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting).
