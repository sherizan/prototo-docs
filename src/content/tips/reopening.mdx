---
title: Open Prototo again
description: "Pick up the same prototype when you come back to your Mac."
---

Open **Prototo** and click your project under **Recent**. That's it: Prototo starts your prototype, brings back the live iPhone, and starts your coding agent in the project, exactly where you left it. You are not starting from scratch, just waking it back up.

Working on something else? Use **File › Open Recent** in the menu bar, or the project name at the top of the window to switch projects.

<Note>
Prototo also reopens your last project on its own when you launch it.
</Note>
