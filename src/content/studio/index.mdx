---
title: "Studio"
description: "Turn a Simulator recording into a polished clip wrapped in a 3D iPhone."
---

Prototo Studio turns a plain Simulator recording into something you'd actually post. Record your prototype, and Studio wraps the clip in a 3D iPhone you can angle and export — ready for a slide, a tweet, or a Slack message.

You never leave the flow you're already in. Record from the terminal, and Studio opens in your browser to finish the clip.

See [Record and wrap](/studio/recording) to make your first one.
