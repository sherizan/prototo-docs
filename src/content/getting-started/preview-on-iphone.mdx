Simulator only gets you so far. A prototype that lives in your hand feels different than one on your trackpad: real iOS 26 Liquid Glass, system haptics, real gestures, real one-handed reach. Test it the way your users will actually use it.

First, get the free **[Prototo app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6772877748)** from the App Store on your iPhone and sign in.

## Live preview while you build

While `proto start` is running (Prototo Desktop runs it for you), your terminal shows a QR code. In the Prototo app, tap **Scan** and point it at that QR. Your prototype loads on the phone and picks up changes as you keep describing screens on the Mac.

- **Keep Prototo running on your Mac.** This preview is your live session.
- **Same Wi-Fi.** iPhone and Mac on the same network.

## Published preview

Run `npx proto share` (or click **Publish** in Prototo Desktop) and open the link on your phone: tap it, scan the QR with the camera, or paste it into **Open a link** in the app. This one is published to the cloud, so it works from anywhere and keeps working after you close Prototo. Details in [Share your prototype](/sharing/share-link).

## A few notes

- **iOS 26+.** Required for Liquid Glass and other system materials.
- The same app is what people you [share with](/sharing/share-link) use to view your prototypes.
