Simulator only gets you so far. A prototype that lives in your hand feels different than one on your trackpad: real iOS 26 Liquid Glass, system haptics, real gestures, real one-handed reach. Test it the way your users will actually use it.

<Note>
The Prototo iPhone app is in App Store review. Until it's approved, the fastest way onto your phone is a **share link**: it opens right in Safari, nothing to install. Want the native app now? You can still [install it directly](#install-the-app-directly) while we wait on Apple.
</Note>

## Open a share link in Safari (no install)

From your project on Mac, run:

```bash
proto share
```

Prototo prints a link and a QR code. Point your iPhone camera at the QR, or send the link to yourself and open it, and your prototype loads in the browser. Tap through it like a real app. No account, no build, no waiting on Apple.

Keep Prototo running while you view it: the preview streams from your session. Full details in [Share a live preview](/sharing/share-link).

## Install the app directly

Prefer the native app, with real haptics and system materials? You can install the current build straight to your device. If any step below won't complete (a new device sometimes needs a fresh build first), use the share link above in the meantime.

### Step 1 — Register your device (one time)

Open this page on your iPhone if you're reading on desktop, then tap the button below.

[Register device →](https://prototo.app/install/ios)

Tap **Register this device** and follow the iOS prompt. Settings will open automatically, tap **Allow**, then **Install**. You'll land on a **"device is ready"** screen. That's expected, it only confirms your device is registered.

<Note>
Don't tap **builds page** on that screen. That's the project dashboard and it asks you to sign in, you don't need an Expo account. Just come back here and continue to Step 2.
</Note>

Takes about 30 seconds. You only do this once.

### Step 2 — Install the Prototo app

Open the install link on your iPhone and tap **Install** on the build page, no sign-in. The app downloads and appears on your home screen.

[Install the app →](https://prototo.app/install/ios/app)

### Step 3 — Preview your prototype

In your Prototo project on Mac, run:

```bash
npx proto start
```

Open Camera on your iPhone, point it at the QR in your terminal, tap the notification, and your prototype loads in Prototo.

## A few notes

- **Same Wi-Fi.** iPhone and Mac on the same network when previewing over `proto start`.
- **iOS 26+.** Required for Liquid Glass and other system materials.
- **No sign-in needed.** The install link is public. If a page asks you to log in, you tapped **builds page** by mistake, back out and use the install link.
- **Just registered a new device?** It may need a fresh build before the app will install. Give it a few minutes and try again, or use a share link while you wait.
