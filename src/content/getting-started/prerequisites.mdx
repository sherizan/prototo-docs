Prototo runs on a Mac with Apple silicon. Everything else, the app sets up with you.

## Step 1: Download Prototo Desktop

[Download Prototo for Mac](https://prototo.app/download), open it, and drag it to Applications.

## Step 2: Let it check your Mac

The first time it opens, Prototo checks for everything it needs and walks you through anything missing, one item at a time:

- **Xcode.** Apple's developer tool. Prototo uses its iOS Simulator, the iPhone preview that runs on your Mac. You never open Xcode itself. It's free from the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/xcode/id497799835).
- **The iOS Simulator.** Installed alongside Xcode.
- **Node.js.** A small runtime your prototypes run on. Free from [nodejs.org](https://nodejs.org/en/download).
- **A coding agent.** It turns plain language into working screens. Prototo works with Claude Code (recommended, from Anthropic) and OpenAI Codex. Pick the one you already pay for: Prototo runs on the AI subscription you already have and never charges for AI use.

Each item shows a tick once it's ready. Anything missing comes with the exact step to fix it, then Prototo checks again.

<Note>
Xcode is about 8GB. Plan for 20 to 40 minutes depending on your connection.
</Note>

Using Claude Code? See [Configure Claude Code](/getting-started/configure-claude-code) for optional extras.

## Step 3: Create your first project

Click **New project**, give it a name, and Prototo sets it up and opens it: your agent on the left, a live iPhone on the right with the starter screen. That's it. You're ready for [your first prototype](/getting-started/first-prototype).
