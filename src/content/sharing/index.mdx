Built a prototype you want to show someone? Click **Publish**.

## Share a link

In Prototo Desktop, click **Publish**. You get a link and a QR code. Anyone with an iPhone opens it with the free Prototo app and runs the real prototype natively on their device. This is the quick path for stakeholders, PMs, and anyone you just want to *show*.

See [Share your prototype](/sharing/share-link).

## Share the flow

Want feedback on the whole thing rather than a tap-through? In the Flow view, click **Export flow** for a link that shows every screen and how they link on a canvas anyone can zoom into, in any browser. See [Prototo Desktop](/getting-started/prototo-desktop).

## Let a teammate build on it

On a Team plan, a teammate can **remix** your prototype into their own copy and keep building. See [Remix a teammate's prototype](/sharing/remix).

---

Sharing needs a free account. Your first publish starts a 7-day Publish trial; building prototypes on your Mac is always free and unlimited. See [Plans and limits](/sharing/plans).
