---

You need a free account to share prototypes. Building on your Mac never needs an account: only sharing does.

## Sign in

You don't need to do anything special: the first time you click **Publish** or **Record**, Prototo opens your browser to sign in. Use **email** (you get a magic link) or **Google**, then come back to Prototo. To sign in ahead of time, choose **Prototo › Sign In…** in the menu bar.

## Manage your shares

Everything lives at [prototo.app/account](https://prototo.app/account):

- Your published prototypes (links are permanent)
- Your Studio recordings
- Your current plan, and the option to upgrade

In Prototo, the **Prototo** menu shows who you're signed in as, opens your account page, and lets you change the name your shares carry.

## Sign out

Choose **Prototo › Sign Out** in the menu bar. The next time you publish or record, Prototo signs you back in.
