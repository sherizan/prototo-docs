---
title: "Plans and limits"
description: "Building locally is free and unlimited. Plans are for publishing."
---

Building prototypes locally is always **free and unlimited**. You can make as many prototypes as you want, with as many screens as you want, forever, without an account.

Plans only come in when you **publish** a prototype as a link.

## Free, Plus, and Team

| | Free | Plus | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $8 / month or $80 / year | $16 per seat / month or $160 per seat / year |
| Build locally | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Publishing | 7-day trial, no card needed | Unlimited | Unlimited, every seat |
| Published links | Permanent | Permanent | Permanent |
| Studio recordings | 30s, 1080p, watermarked | 180s, 4K, GIF, no watermark | 180s, 4K, GIF, no watermark |
| Shared library | — | — | Everyone on the team sees each other's published prototypes |
| Billing | — | Your card | One invoice for the whole team |

Everyone starts on Free. Your first publish quietly starts a 7-day trial. There is no card, no countdown, no nag. Publish as much as you like during those 7 days. Upgrade any time at [prototo.app/pricing](https://prototo.app/pricing) or from your [account](https://prototo.app/account).

## Teams

One person starts the team from their [account](https://prototo.app/account): name it, pick how many seats you need at checkout, then invite designers by their email address. Anyone who already has a Prototo account is on the team right away. Anyone who doesn't gets an email, and joins the moment they sign in with that address.

Every seat gets everything Plus unlocks. The published prototypes of everyone on the team show up in one shared library on each member's account page, so a review is one link away. Only the person who published a prototype can delete it.

Need more seats, an invoice, or to cancel? That all lives under **Manage billing** on the team owner's account page. If a seat holder also has a personal Plus plan, cancel it there too so you don't pay twice.

## How viewing works

People you share with run your prototype natively with the free [Prototo app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6772877748) on their iPhone. Viewing is free and unlimited for them, and it never counts against your plan. Every link you publish stays live forever, even if you never upgrade.

## When the trial ends

After the 7 days, publishing new links asks you to upgrade. Everything you already published keeps working:

```
Your 7-day Publish trial has ended. Upgrade to Plus ($8/mo or $80/yr)
to keep publishing.
  Upgrade at https://prototo.app/pricing
```

If you only need to hand the project to someone on a Mac, [Share via GitHub](/sharing/share-github) has no limits.
