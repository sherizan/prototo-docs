---
title: "Plans and limits"
description: "Building locally is free and unlimited. Plans are for sharing."
---

Building prototypes locally is always **free and unlimited**. You can make as many prototypes as you want, with as many screens as you want, forever, without an account.

Plans only come in when you **share** a prototype as a link.

## Free vs Plus

| | Free | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $8 / month |
| Build locally | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Share at a time | 3 prototypes | 10 prototypes |
| Share links last | 7 days | 30 days |
| Studio recordings | 30s, 1080p, watermarked | 180s, 4K, GIF and 3D tilt, no watermark |

Everyone starts on Free. Upgrade any time at [prototo.app/pricing](https://prototo.app/pricing) or from your [account](https://prototo.app/account).

## How viewing works

People you share with run your prototype natively with the free [Prototo app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6772877748) on their iPhone. Viewing is free and unlimited for them, and it never counts against your plan. Your limits are only about how many prototypes you keep shared at once and how long the links live.

## Hitting a limit

If you're already sharing your maximum number of prototypes, `npx proto share` opens your account page so you can upgrade or free up a slot:

```
You're at your free sharing limit. Opening prototo.app/account —
upgrade there to keep sharing more.
```

If you only need to hand the project to someone on a Mac, [Share via GitHub](/sharing/share-github) has no limits.
