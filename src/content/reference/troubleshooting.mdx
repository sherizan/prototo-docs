title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Most problems have a one-click fix."
---

The rule: no error shown to you requires engineering knowledge to interpret. When Prototo can't recover on its own, it tells you what to do.

## What you see, and what to do

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A component couldn't be found | Click **Reload** in the toolbar under the iPhone |
| A screen has an error | Ask your agent: `fix any errors in the [ScreenName] screen` |
| Prototo is already running | Another Prototo window has the Simulator. Quit it, then try again |
| Prototo needs to update | Nothing to do: Prototo updates itself when you open a project |
| Can't reach your device | Check your iPhone and Mac are on the same Wi-Fi (or use your iPhone's hotspot, see [Preview on iPhone](/getting-started/preview-on-iphone)) |
| Something went wrong | Click **Reload** in the toolbar under the iPhone |

## The escape hatch: Reload

Click **Reload** in the toolbar under the iPhone. This fixes most stuck states: Prototo clears its caches and restarts your prototype, then the preview comes back.

If taps stop landing on the preview but the picture keeps moving, choose **View › Restart Preview** instead.

## A screen has an error

If your agent writes a screen that won't run, Prototo tells you which one. Ask your agent:

```
fix any errors in the [ScreenName] screen
```

It reads the file, finds the issue, and rewrites it, without you seeing the underlying error.

## The iPhone preview isn't showing

Prototo checks Xcode and the iOS Simulator when it starts. If something is missing, it shows the step to fix it. If everything is installed and the preview still doesn't appear, choose **View › Restart Preview**.

## My agent isn't reading my design system

AGENTS.md and DESIGN.md must be at the root of your project folder, not inside a subfolder. Prototo starts your agent in the project folder for you, so it picks them up automatically.

## A custom component library isn't generating the right screens

Add 2 or 3 component examples to an `## Examples` section in DESIGN.md, then ask your agent to regenerate the screen. Two or three examples cover most libraries. It works out the rest.

## I need to start fresh

Click **New project** in Prototo and give it a new name. Your old project stays in its folder until you delete it from Finder.

## Still stuck?

Reach out via [prototo.app](https://prototo.app).
